KosherGuard support

KosherGuard is invitation-only during its pilot. Support is handled through these channels:

  • In-product support requests - sign in to the family portal and open a support request: account recovery, enrollment assistance, device connectivity troubleshooting, policy-change requests, or a general question.
  • Verified email support (planned) - the verified support address will be provided to pilot families with their invitation once the support mailbox is configured; it is not a monitored channel yet.
  • Escalation - requests that need it are escalated to an authorized administrator.

Support cannot casually remove device protection: policy changes always go through the protected review flow by an authorized guardian.

We do not advertise support hours or 24/7 service during the pilot; responses are best-effort.
