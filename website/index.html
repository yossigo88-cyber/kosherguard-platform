Family-safe internet, managed from day one.

The best filtering solution for our technical generation.

KosherGuard is building its own filtering engine and managed iPhone protection, with family-set policies enforced by our network edge. We are in development and not yet launched.

How it works

Families enroll supervised iPhones, choose policies per device, and KosherGuard compiles those policies to its filtering edge. Changes publish only after review, and every change is audited. A device policy can never be made less restrictive than the protected family baseline by accident.

Own filtering engine

Website and app filtering by category and domain, compiled and published to our DNS and proxy edge.

Designed for supervised enrollment

Our goal is enrollment through Apple Automated Device Enrollment, so management cannot simply be switched off. This capability is subject to Apple approval and has not yet been validated on real devices.

Family control

Multiple guardians per family, per-device policies, and a clear audit trail of every change.

Protected baseline

KosherGuard administrators control a protected baseline. Families manage delegated settings and approved exceptions; ordinary dashboard controls cannot weaken that baseline.

Contact

KosherGuard is invitation-only during its pilot. To request an invitation or ask a question, use the support request option inside the family portal, or reach the team through the support channel listed on the support page.
