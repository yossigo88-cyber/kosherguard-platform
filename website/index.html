Family-safe internet, managed from day one.

KosherGuard is building its own filtering engine and managed iPhone protection, with family-set policies enforced by our network edge. We are in development and not yet launched.

Own filtering engine

Website and app filtering by category and domain, compiled and published to our DNS and proxy edge.

Designed for supervised enrollment

Our goal is enrollment through Apple Automated Device Enrollment, so management cannot simply be switched off. This capability is subject to Apple approval and has not yet been validated on real devices.

Family control

Multiple guardians per family, per-device policies, and a clear audit trail of every change.

Tighten-only policies

A device policy can never be made less restrictive than the family default by accident.

KosherGuard is under active development. Nothing on this page is a live service yet; device management features described here are goals pending Apple approval, enrollment eligibility, and device testing.
