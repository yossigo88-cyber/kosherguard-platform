Enrollment information

KosherGuard is designed for supervised iPhone enrollment, so that protection cannot simply be switched off. The planned flow:

  1. Your family account is set up by invitation.
  2. Each device gets a short-lived, single-use enrollment ticket.
  3. The device enrolls through Apple Automated Device Enrollment (ADE) and checks in; management verifies the enrollment before any protection is considered active.

Honest limitations

Supervised enrollment depends on Apple approval and enrollment eligibility and has not yet been validated on real devices. Restoring or erasing a device involves Apple-side steps - Apple Business assignment, Find My, Activation Lock, backups and eSIM behavior - that KosherGuard does not control and does not promise to perform. A restored device is not guaranteed to activate when Apple services, network access, credentials or account ownership prevent it.
