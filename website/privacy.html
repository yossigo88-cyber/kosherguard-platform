KosherGuard privacy notice

DRAFT - pending professional legal review. This page is a working draft prepared for review. It is not legal advice, has not been approved by a qualified professional, and does not represent a final privacy policy. Before launch it requires review for the actual customer model, children's data, the relevant jurisdictions, Apple requirements, and any future TLS inspection.

What we aim to collect

Only what managing and securing a device requires: the device identifier needed for enrollment, model, OS version, supervision/enrollment and MDM status, managed-app status where needed, policy and configuration versions, last check-in time, and relevant certificate status. We do not collect device location or an inventory of personal apps by default.

Children's data

KosherGuard is designed for families. The treatment of children's data, consent, and data-processing terms is a legal question under review; this draft makes no legal claims about them.

Filtering logs

Filtering decisions exist so families can enforce their policies. KosherGuard stores only blocked decisions, and only the blocked domain - never full URLs, page paths or free-text reasons. Allowed browsing history is never stored, even if a device submits it. Blocked entries are physically deleted, not merely hidden, after a retention period the family configures between 1 and 30 days; the default is 7 days. Viewing the log requires an authenticated family account and is limited to that family's data. Routine database backups exclude filtering history and restores never bring it back; any archives outside the service's own systems are the operator's responsibility under the pending legal review.
